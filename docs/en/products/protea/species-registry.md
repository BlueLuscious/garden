# Protea Species Registry

Status: **Canonical Protea species authority; no assignments**

This registry owns species-level botanical identities inside Protea. The
[Garden Genus Registry](../../registry/genera.md) owns the *Protea* genus assignment.

No canonical species-to-internal-identity mapping is currently established. A future assignment
must identify a durable Protea concept, preserve accepted taxonomy, and remain distinct from
ordinary component families, behavioural variants, and presentation styles unless one of those
concepts proves sufficiently stable to warrant a botanical identity.
