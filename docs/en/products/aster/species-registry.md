# Aster Species Registry

Status: **Canonical Aster species authority**

This registry maps botanical species of *Aster* to official named identities owned by the Aster
product. The [Garden Genus Registry](../../registry/genera.md) owns the genus assignment; this
document owns species-level assignments inside Aster.

## Mapping

Official Aster icon collections may be named after species of *Aster*. A species identifies the
collection, while visual rules, icon membership, package behaviour, and release status remain
Aster product concerns.

## Assignments

### Aster amellus L.

- **Status:** Used
- **Assigned Identity:** Amellus foundational icon collection
- **Taxonomic Rank:** Species
- **Taxonomic Reference:** [Kew Plants of the World Online](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A331070-2/general-information)
- **Type Reference:** [International Plant Names Index](https://www.ipni.org/n/331068-2)
- **Meaning / Rationale:** The designated type of *Aster* names the first representative,
  minimalist, general-purpose Aster icon collection.
- **Taxonomic Notes:** `Amellus` is also an accepted genus name. This assignment always denotes
  the complete species *Aster amellus* and reserves the short product-context identity within
  Garden while it remains Used.
- **Product Documentation:** [Amellus Collection](https://github.com/BlueLuscious/aster/tree/develop/docs/en/collections/amellus)

## Assignment Policy

Species are selected when an official collection is designed or established, rather than by
reserving a complete species catalogue in advance. The botanical identity must emerge from the
actual character and purpose of the collection.

Future entries record:

```text
Scientific Name: Aster <species>
Status: Proposed | Used | Retired
Assigned Identity: <Aster collection>
Meaning / Rationale: <why the species fits the collection>
Taxonomic Notes: <synonym, accepted placement, or historical classification if relevant>
```
